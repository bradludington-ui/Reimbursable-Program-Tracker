Customers

The master list of who reimburses you. Customers are not tied to a fiscal year — the same customer is referenced by cost shares and funding documents in every year. Retiring a customer keeps every historical reference intact; it only stops the customer being offered on new share lines.

Cost shares

A cost share is a named, reusable table of customer → percentage, held for one fiscal year. Attach it to as many reimbursable groups as you like — edit it once and every group using it follows. A table can be entered as percentages directly, or as driver quantities (aircraft, flight hours, personnel) that the tool converts to percentages. Percentages are only ever read from one place, so a group can never disagree with its own share table.

Invoices

An invoice is raised against one effort: you give it a month and a total, and the cost-shared amounts fill in from that effort's table. Those amounts are then saved onto the invoice — editing the cost share later never rewrites what an invoice already billed. Where a table has moved since, the invoice says so.
